Rocq (formerly Coq) proof term stepper

Every Rocq tactic builds part of a λ-term. This free tool runs a proof in your browser and shows, step by step, how intro, apply, exact, reflexivity, destruct, induction, split, left, right, assert, rewrite, assumption, discriminate, revert and exists construct the proof term. All terms are real Show Proof. output.

Browse one example per tactic or enable JavaScript to step through a proof.
